# The workbench

Click an episode on Review, or **Review stills** on Jobs, to open its
workbench: every picked still in time order, each with its marks and its
buttons.

![An episode's workbench: stills with cull, keep, lock, hide and edit labels buttons](/shots/workbench.avif "The workbench for Charge. Frames (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org.")

## One still at a time

Under each still: **cull** (or **restore**), **keep**, **lock** (or
**unlock**), **hide** (or **show**) and **edit labels**. A button acts in
place, and the page keeps your position.

The row of filters above the stills narrows the sheet: all, unreviewed,
culled, hidden, and when there are any, repeats, not picked, and the
[pool](pool.md).

## Several at once

Tick the stills' boxes. Shift-click ticks a run, which is how you take an
opening or ending in one go. The bar that appears does cull, keep,
restore, lock, unlock, hide and show to every ticked still.

## Keys

When no field has focus. Press <kbd>j</kbd> first to pick a still; the
other keys act on the outlined one.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> | next / previous still |
| <kbd>space</kbd> | tick the current still (<kbd>shift</kbd> + <kbd>space</kbd> for a run) |
| <kbd>c</kbd> | cull it, or restore it if it is culled |
| <kbd>v</kbd> | keep it |
| <kbd>e</kbd> | open its label editor |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | clear the ticks |

**Mark the rest kept** is here too, for this episode only. Here it has no
Undo, so set stills back with the bar's **restore**.

For an episode, the arrows under the title walk the season. The
**episode title** fold lets you type a title of your own; it shows
instead of Shoko's and survives re-runs. **Re-read from Shoko** fetches
Shoko's title again, for an episode Shoko had no title for at the time.

## Correcting labels

**edit labels** (or <kbd>e</kbd>) opens the still's editor.

![The label editor: each label family with the model's proposal and a choice](/shots/editor.avif "The label editor. Frame (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org.")

- **Labels**: each family shows the model's proposal. Leave it on **as
  proposed**, pick another value, pick **none** to clear it, or type a
  new value. A typed value becomes a choice for every other still.
- **Tags**: untick a tag to remove it; type more, separated by commas.
- **Review**: set unreviewed, keep or cull, lock it, hide it, and leave a
  note for yourself.

Your corrections sit beside the model's values rather than over them, so
a re-run with a new model still shows yours.

Up to four buttons save: **Save and next** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>;
not on the last still), **Save and next unreviewed** (while there is a
later unreviewed still), **Save and stay** and **Save and back to the
sheet**. <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> walk
without saving, and <kbd>n</kbd> jumps to the next unreviewed still.
Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.

## A post card

**make a post card**, at the top of the editor, opens a page that draws
the still on a paper or dark matte with its palette and the title, in
portrait (4:5), square or 16:9. Two switches take the shadow, midtone and
highlight swatches and the line of labels off the card. **Save PNG**
downloads it. It is drawn in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
