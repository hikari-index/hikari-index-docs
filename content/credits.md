# Credits

## Films in the screenshots

Every frame shown in these docs comes from Blender Studio's open films,
used under
[Creative Commons Attribution](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/) (CC BY)
licenses: credit the Blender Foundation and you may reuse them. The screenshots were made by running the films through Hikari
Index; the frames are as the gallery shows them: scaled and encoded as AVIF,
inside the gallery's own pages.

- **Big Buck Bunny** (2008). (CC) Blender Foundation |
  [studio.blender.org](https://studio.blender.org/projects/big-buck-bunny/).
- **Charge** (2022). (CC) Blender Foundation |
  [studio.blender.org](https://studio.blender.org/projects/charge/).
- **Hero** (2018), the Grease Pencil showcase. (CC) Blender Foundation |
  [studio.blender.org](https://studio.blender.org/projects/hero/).

Blender Studio makes these films in the open and releases them for
anyone to reuse. If you need test material for your own install, they
are a good place to start.
